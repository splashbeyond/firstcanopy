Why Leaf Group exists

Most deal flow is already picked over by the time it reaches your inbox.

Auction processes reward speed, tolerance for competition, and willingness to stretch. Leaf Group exists for buyers that need a quieter path: a repeatable origination function built around their own acquisition thesis, not a subscription feed of broadly marketed opportunities.

We translate investment criteria into a live target universe, research ownership and strategic fit, engage decision makers directly, and qualify seller motivation before a buyer spends time on a conversation.

Origination mandate

A retained sourcing function calibrated to the buyer’s actual investment criteria.

Leaf Group is not a general deal-flow subscription. Each engagement begins with a mandate memo that defines the target universe, qualification logic, outreach posture, and reporting cadence.

01

Thesis translation

02

Counterparty research

03

Direct owner outreach

Qualified handoff

Institutional discipline

Built for buyers who already know what they want to own.

The firm works best when the mandate is specific: a fragmented market, a defined buyer advantage, and a clear reason why an owner would take a private conversation seriously.

  • Platform and add-on searches in fragmented lower-middle-market categories
  • Founder-owned, family-owned, sponsor-backed, and corporate divestiture targets
  • Ongoing target review, outreach reporting, and qualification notes
  • Buyer-controlled pacing, confidentiality, and introduction standards

Operating model

From investment thesis to qualified acquisition conversation.

The work is structured as a disciplined operating cadence, giving the buyer visibility into target logic, outreach activity, seller feedback, and the next best action.

01

Mandate definition

02

Universe build

For business owners

If Leaf Group contacted you, it was for a specific buyer’s acquisition thesis.

We represent the buyer in these conversations. Initial discussions are exploratory and confidential, and there is no obligation to continue. The purpose is to understand whether the buyer’s strategy and your company’s objectives may justify a more formal conversation.

Leaf Group does not present outreach as a generic valuation exercise, market check, or brokered auction. We will be direct about why your company was identified, who we represent at the appropriate stage, and what a next step would require.
